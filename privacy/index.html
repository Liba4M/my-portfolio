Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Liba Mahmood ("I", "me") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use this website (byliba.co) or my private tutoring services.

Who I am

I'm an independent private tutor based in the UK, providing 1:1 online GCSE and A-Level Maths and Computer Science tuition. You can contact me at libam1714@gmail.com.

What information I collect

  • Enquiry form: name, email, phone number, subject, year group, preferred contact method, and any details you share about learning needs.
  • Direct contact: if you email, WhatsApp, or call me directly, I hold whatever information you choose to share (e.g. your child's name, year group, exam board).
  • Website usage: this site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind.

How I use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange lessons
  • To plan and personalise tutoring content for each student
  • To process payments and keep basic records for tax purposes

I do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who I share it with

I use a small number of trusted services to run my tutoring business, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Meet / Gmail — for lesson delivery and email communication
  • Calendly — for lesson scheduling
  • FormSubmit.co — to process the enquiry form on this site and forward it to my email

I don't use any other third-party analytics or advertising services on this site.

Children's information

As tuition is often for students under 18, enquiries and lesson details may include a child's name and academic information, submitted by a parent or guardian on the child's behalf. This information is used only to provide tutoring and is not shared beyond what's necessary to deliver lessons.

How long I keep information

I retain enquiry and lesson records for as long as needed to provide tutoring and meet basic accounting/tax obligations (typically up to 6 years), after which they are deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask me to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time — just email libam1714@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be published at this page.
