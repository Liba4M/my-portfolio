About Liba

I'm a recent BSc. Computer Science Graduate from the University of Birmingham and a diligent online tutor with over 20 five-star reviews from students and parents. I know exactly how overwhelming GCSEs and A-Levels can feel, so I focus on breaking each topic down into clear, manageable steps, building confidence alongside knowledge.

With over 3 years of experience, I can streamline your learning to get you to the result you want. I have helped students achieve grade 9s from grade 6s and achieve A*/A.

My lessons are patient, exam-focused, and tailored to how each student learns best, whether that's working through past papers together, revisiting fundamentals, or tackling the topics that feel hardest first.

Aside from tutoring, I work as a Graduate Technology Consultant. I love oil-painting, travelling and shopping!

How Lessons Work

A structured, proven process from your first lesson to exam day.

1

Personalised Learning Plan

I identify knowledge gaps and build a plan around your target grades and deadlines, whether catching up, strengthening foundations, or aiming for the top.

2

Clear Explanations

Complex topics broken into simple steps with real-world examples, focused on the "why" behind concepts, not just memorising methods.

3

Exam-Focused Practice

Having recently sat the GCSE and A-Level exams myself, I know exactly what examiners want: past papers, technique, and detailed feedback.

4

Active Learning

I use proven methods like active recall, spaced repetition, and interactive quizzes, the same techniques that helped me succeed academically.

5

Progress Tracking

Personalised feedback, progress reports, and tailored homework, powered by my own platform, TutorGenie.
