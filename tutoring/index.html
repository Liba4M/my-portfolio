About Liba

I'm a recent Computer Science graduate and a passionate online tutor with over 20 five-star reviews from students and parents. I know exactly how overwhelming GCSEs can feel — so I focus on breaking each topic down into clear, manageable steps, building confidence alongside knowledge.

My lessons are patient, exam-focused, and tailored to how each student learns best — whether that's working through past papers together, revisiting fundamentals, or tackling the topics that feel hardest first.

I hold an enhanced DBS certificate, so parents can feel completely confident having me work with their child.
