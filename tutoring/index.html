I know exactly how overwhelming GCSEs and A-Levels can feel — so I focus on breaking each topic down into clear, manageable steps, building confidence alongside knowledge.

How Lessons Work

1. Personalised Learning Plan Before diving into topics, I analyse each student’s current understanding, identify knowledge gaps, and create a personalised learning plan designed around their target grades and deadlines. Whether you need to catch up on missed topics, strengthen foundations, prepare for exams, or push towards the highest grades, every session is planned with your progress in mind.

2. Clear Explanations & Confidence Building Complex topics are broken down into simple, manageable steps using clear explanations, visual examples, and real-world connections. I focus on helping students understand the “why” behind concepts, not just memorising methods. My goal is to turn difficult topics into something approachable and help students develop the confidence to solve problems independently.

3. Exam-Focused Practice & Techniques Having recently completed the GCSE and A-Level systems myself, I understand exactly what examiners are looking for and how students can maximise their marks. Lessons include targeted exam-style questions, past paper practice, and detailed feedback to improve accuracy, structure, and exam technique. I teach students how to recognise question patterns, avoid common mistakes, and approach exams strategically.

4. Active Learning & Science-Based Methods I use proven learning techniques including active recall, spaced repetition, interactive quizzes, and targeted practice — methods that helped me achieve my own academic success. Instead of passively listening, students actively engage with the material, improving understanding, memory retention, and long-term confidence.

5. Continuous Support & Progress Tracking My tutoring goes beyond the lesson itself. I provide personalised feedback, progress reports, and carefully selected homework based on each student’s weaknesses and goals. I continuously adapt lessons based on performance, ensuring students are always working on the areas that will have the biggest impact on their results. To maximise lesson efficiency, I also use my own educational platform, TutorGenie, designed to support personalised learning, track progress, and help students reach their goals faster through smarter tutoring.
